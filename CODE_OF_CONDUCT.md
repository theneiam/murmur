# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome
newcomers, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks,
and discriminatory language are not tolerated anywhere the project operates —
issues, pull requests, discussions, or elsewhere.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/theneiam) (profile → contact options) rather than
in a public thread. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or lock content and may temporarily or permanently exclude anyone who
violates this code.
